STEP 04

Meet with AE

A fast daily sync to stay aligned with your AE. Come ready to cover:

  • ●
    Priority accountsWhich target accounts need attention today or this week
  • ●
    Objection intelPatterns you're hearing on calls that the AE should know about
  • ●
    Joint outreach strategyCoordinating who touches which contact, and when
  • ●
    Collaborative email follow-upsAligning on messaging for shared threads

Meeting Cadence

SDR paired with two AEs

Meet with one AE on Monday and Wednesday, and the other on Tuesday and Thursday. The first meeting of the week should run about 30 minutes as a strategic planning session. The second meeting can be a 10–15 minute sync to check on progress and see where support is needed in either direction.

Two SDRs paired with one AE

All three should sync twice a week: 30 minutes at the start of the week for strategic planning, and 15 minutes later in the week as a check-in/update.

Intent Signals

Both AEs and SDRs should dive into ZoomInfo for intent signals — hiring surges, job changes, and leadership moves at target accounts often point to a reason to reach out now.
